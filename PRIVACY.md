# Privacy

Ink Notes keeps everything on your device.

- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no crash reporting.**
- Notebooks, books, recordings and settings are stored in the app's own folder on the tablet. Backups (`.inkz` files) are created only when you ask for one.
- **The only network use is the optional AI feature.** It is off until you open *Settings > AI service* and enter your own API key (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter). Only then, when you tap an AI action, the page, text or audio you chose is sent straight from the tablet to the provider you picked, under that provider's terms. Nothing goes through any server of ours; there is none.
- Your API key is stored privately inside the app on your tablet. It is not written to logs and not included in `.inkz` backups.
- Permissions: *Microphone* (only while you record), *Storage* (to import and export files), *Internet* (the AI feature only).
